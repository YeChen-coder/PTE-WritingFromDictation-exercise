The whole thing started because the author wanted the practice flow to be faster, without having to click several times after every dictation item.

So the workflow became this: the app contains more than 100 Write from Dictation questions. The audio plays once, the user types the sentence, and when they feel done, they can simply press Enter. If the answer is completely correct, the app automatically moves to the next question. If there is any mistake, the feedback area in the middle compares the answer word by word and directly marks where the errors are.

In addition, the question numbers for mistakes are recorded automatically. You can also practice only the wrong questions by using the "Start Mistake Training" option on the left.

One thing to pay special attention to:
while the program is open, mistakes will keep accumulating, but they are not persistently saved. If you close the whole program, restart the computer, or reopen the browser, the previously recorded wrong-question numbers will no longer be available.

The "Practice Controls" section on the left includes, but is not limited to:

1. The countdown time after the audio ends
2. Whether to automatically move to the next question after a completely correct answer
3. Whether to play motivational sound effects, as well as the volume settings for different sounds

All of these can be changed. The motivational sound effect exists simply because the author personally needs a bit of encouragement, so they found an audio clip and used it as a reward sound. Everyone can replace it with whatever they like. It really does not matter.
